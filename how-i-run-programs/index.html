Method

How I run programs

The Stablecoin Labs job is a lifecycle: discovery, POC with success criteria, validation, then a scale or no-scale business case. Here is CryptoPos run through that lifecycle as if it were a funded program — gates, dependencies, risks, KPIs, governance. Diagrams over paragraphs.

Planning artifact — illustrative program, real method

Milestone map

Four gates. Each has a door that closes.

Entry criteria stop a stage starting on vibes; exit criteria stop it ending on momentum. Durations are illustrative planning values, not a record of anything that ran.

Discovery

≈ 4 weeks (demo)

Entry
Named merchant segment where card economics pinch (small-ticket, high-frequency)
Problem hypothesis written before any solution work
Exit
20+ merchant interviews synthesised; acceptance blockers ranked by cost, not anecdote
Success criteria for the POC agreed before the POC exists

Kill here if: the pain ranks below #3 for the segment.

POC

≈ 6 weeks (demo)

Entry
Success criteria signed; dependency owners named (see graph below)
Compliance review scheduled, not assumed
Exit
Working prototype covering charge, settle, refund — the unhappy path included
Unit economics modelled per segment against the card benchmark

Kill here if: modelled all-in cost can’t beat cards for any real segment.

Validation

≈ 8–12 weeks (demo)

Entry
Pilot cohort sized for signal, instrumented for the KPI tree
Risk register mitigations in place for every high-likelihood row
Exit
North-star read at day 90 with confidence intervals, not anecdotes
Dispute & refund incidents documented with resolution times

Kill here if: repeat acceptance decays past week 6 despite incentives off.

Scale / no-scale

Decision, not a phase

Entry
Validation data complete; costs restated with pilot actuals
Exit
A business case that survives finance review — or a written no-scale memo with what we learned and what would change the answer

A well-argued no is a success outcome. Zombie pilots are the failure mode.

Dependency graph

The POC is the easy node.

The sequencing insight most POCs miss: the compliance review is the longest pole, so it starts first and its findings feed partner selection — not the other way round. Chain finality and tax reporting don’t block the build, but they bound what the pilot may promise merchants.

KPI tree

One north star, four drivers, named events.

Every leaf carries its instrumentation — a metric without an event name is a wish.

Day-90 active acceptance ★ merchants transacting weekly, one quarter in, incentives off
  • Activation onboarded → first live charge; friction diagnostic merchant_onboarded · first_charge_settled · time-to-first-charge
  • Settlement mix share kept as stablecoin vs auto-converted — revealed trust settlement_policy_set · sweep_executed{policy, threshold}
  • Rewards loop health redemption rate inside margin floor = the incentive is working and affordable reward_accrued · reward_redeemed · margin_floor_hit
  • Dispute & refund load leading indicator of the missing rail hurting; rises before losses do refund_requested{unit} · dispute_opened · resolution_time
