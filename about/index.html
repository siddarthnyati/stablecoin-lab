About

Siddarth Nyati

Eager to learn. Excited to be challenged. Unafraid to struggle. I turn any problem into a working product — bring it on.

Six years of product management across public-sector tax (Infosys), a regulated healthcare marketplace (ResMed ecosystem), and payments infrastructure (Visa) — every one of them “translate complex rules into running products.” Today I work on Visa’s 3DS platform — the layer of card rails that decides whether a credential is real and whether the person presenting it is who they claim. My last year there has been network tokenization, Visa Payment Passkey on FIDO/WebAuthn, enriched authorization data, and the acceptance economics that make merchants say yes. (Whenever this site says “tokenization” about my experience, it means payment-credential tokenization on card rails; on-chain asset tokenization is a subject I study, not one I’ve operated.)

Outside work hours, I build proofs of concept on crypto rails — the five on this site — because I think the interesting stablecoin problems are the ones card networks already solved once, under different names. The way I run that work is documented as its own artifact: a gated lifecycle from discovery to a scale/no-scale decision, with a seven-gate human review process I’ve applied across four platform initiatives and 104 acceptance criteria. The method is here.
