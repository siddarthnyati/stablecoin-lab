About

Siddarth Nyati

Product manager on payment rails; builder of things on the next one.

Concept prototype — illustrative data

I work on Visa’s 3DS platform — the layer of card rails that decides whether a credential is real and whether the person presenting it is who they claim. My last year there has been network tokenization, Visa Payment Passkey on FIDO/WebAuthn, enriched authorization data, and the acceptance economics that make merchants say yes. (Whenever this site says “tokenization” about my experience, it means payment-credential tokenization on card rails; on-chain asset tokenization is a subject I study, not one I’ve operated.)

Outside work hours, I build proofs of concept on crypto rails — the five on this site — because I think the interesting stablecoin problems are the ones card networks already solved once, under different names. The way I run that work is documented as its own artifact: a gated lifecycle from discovery to a scale/no-scale decision, with a seven-gate human review process I’ve applied across four platform initiatives and 104 acceptance criteria. The method is here.

Everything on this site follows two rules: nothing implies a POC shipped or had users, and every number is either sourced or labelled as illustrative. If a claim couldn’t be verified, it appears as a visible [[TK]] marker instead of a guess — the open list lives in OPEN_ITEMS.md in this site’s repository.
