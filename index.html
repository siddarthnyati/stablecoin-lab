The journey

Engineering school

Where the building habit started — and the first time software felt like leverage.

Infosys — IRS India’s tax rails

  • Launched an online tax dispute-resolution service — 600K users saved ~$10M in taxes
  • Automated verification for the Indian Revenue Services — 71 tax forms, bug-free portal for 5M+ users

Dartmouth — admit, scholarship, and a lesson in credit

The bank wanted our home — worth 5× the loan — as collateral at 12% APR, while a stock portfolio worth more than the loan counted for nothing. That loan seeded DeFiLend.

Motional — autonomous vehicles

  • Established an organisation-wide risk framework — 21+ risks documented across 6 teams, with an escalation process for blockers
  • Rider-app POC from infotainment & ride-quality feedback — UI mockups, beta-tested, NPS +20%
  • Operations dashboard on complex vehicle data — 4 new KPIs, software releases 2 hours faster

2023 · ResMed ecosystem — patient resupply

  • Zero-touch reorders for chronic medical supplies — reorder rate 40% → 70%, AOV +42%, fulfilment down 3–5 days
  • POC → scalable platform: interoperability-first architecture with localised compliance — the long-term, forward-looking solution
  • Presented to leadership; 80% of enrolled patients converted to platform reordering

2025 · Visa — Cardinal 3DS platform

  • Led last-mile network-token delivery, definition → launch — integrated Visa TMS so merchants and PSPs exchange card credentials for network tokens; 8B+ transactions brought into tokenization scope, rails laid for agentic commerce (~5% forecast authorization lift)
  • Identified issuers’ e-commerce authorization problems and led DCAP, the enriched-data program — device intelligence and billing signals over Data Only rails in exchange for interchange relief; adoption grew 30% MoM to 1M+ transactions/month
  • Root-cause analysis & customer research on platform revenue leakage — new authorization data lifting e-commerce approval rates +280bps, with +50bps incremental where DCAP standards are met
