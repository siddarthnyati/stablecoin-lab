Who am I

Siddarth Nyati — product manager for regulated money.

Tax portals for 5M+ Indians. Medical resupply for patients who couldn’t wait. Card authentication on Visa’s 3-D Secure platform for everyone else. I build products where the rules are half the product — and I prototype relentlessly on whatever rail comes next.

Stablecoins are a rails change, not a problem change. Three questions decide every payment — credential, person, loss — and I work on the card-rail answers. The five concepts below ask them of stablecoins.

One belief throughout: people don’t adopt financial products they can’t practise first.
